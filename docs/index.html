About Me

Professional Summary

I am AmirReza Azari, a B.Sc. graduate in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology. My focus is on Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL). I am an enthusiastic AI engineer with a strong desire to learn, grow, and contribute to challenging projects. I continuously expand my knowledge through hands-on development and research while building expertise in modern AI technologies.

Life & Interests

Outside of engineering and research, I've probably spent more of my life with books than anything else. Learning has always been a big part of who I am, whether it was preparing for exams, exploring new ideas, or simply satisfying my curiosity.

I'm naturally a quiet and introverted person, and I genuinely enjoy spending time reading, learning, or simply listening to music. Music is my constant companion—I spend hours discovering new songs and curating playlists on my Telegram channel. I also enjoy watching movies from time to time and keeping track of what I've seen on IMDb.

That said, I also love going out, exploring new places, and spending quality time with my close friends. I enjoy working out and staying active, and I'm looking forward to making fitness an even bigger part of my life in the years ahead.
